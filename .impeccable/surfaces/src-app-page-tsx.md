## Direction contract
- **THESIS:** Khatma is a 30-compartment Mamluk Rab'ah chest and architectural manuscript matrix (`الربعة الشريفة والمخطوطة المعمارية`), refusing generic rounded SaaS cards, icon tiles, and kickers above headings.
- **OWN-WORLD:** Deep obsidian-emerald banner (`#091B15`) with an 8-pointed gold Islamic Shamsa rosette (`#C5A059`), a 30-segment brass progress bar (`[✓]`), warm limestone/parchment ground (`#F5F1E8` light / `#07120E` dark), precision `1px` brass-hairline cells (`#C5A059`) with corner `+` registration crosshairs, circular scalloped emerald letterpress seals (`خُتم`) for completed Juz', top chamfered brass tabs (`محجوز`) for reserved Juz', a triple-bordered geometric Mushaf page with 8-pointed gold verse rosettes, and a floating obsidian-emerald pill audio player.
- **STORY:** A visitor opens a shared Khatma link, immediately sees the sacred title, the 30-segment brass completion bar, and the 30 numbered Rab'ah compartments stamped with `خُتم` or `محجوز`, claims an available compartment in one tap, reads the 604-page Uthmani Mushaf while listening to Yasser Al-Dosari or Islam Sobhi, and stamps their Juz' complete.
- **FIRST VIEWPORT:** Obsidian-emerald architectural header with 8-pointed gold Shamsa rosette on the left, large gold calligraphic title (`Amiri`) on the right, and the 30-unit segmented brass progress bar across the bottom of the header, opening directly onto the 5-column architectural hairline matrix of 30 Juz' compartments.
- **FORM:** Candidate 7 (`Architectural Rab'ah & Hairline Matrix`), position 7 on the ordered list, seed key `3c4124c0`, raised by `design-annual-s-plate-section` (1px hairlines, corner registration crosshairs, circular attribution stamp) and `vernacular-ephemera-boarding-pass-and-gate-board` (segmented status bar and live in-place cell state).
